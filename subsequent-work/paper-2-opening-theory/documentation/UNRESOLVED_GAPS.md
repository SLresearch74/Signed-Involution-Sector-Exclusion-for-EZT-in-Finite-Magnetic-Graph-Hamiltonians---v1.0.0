# Unresolved gaps

1. **Effective fixed-weight depth.** The Noetherian proof supplies existence but no computable or useful bound on the largest required coefficient index.
2. **Uniform positive-weight atlas.** No finite global recursive atlas over all positive weights is proved.
3. **Stable family extension.** It is unknown which portions of the 16-cone fan persist under structured perturbations of the off-diagonal Hamiltonian.
4. **Beyond analytic arcs.** Smooth-flat germs require a different framework; Taylor order alone is insufficient.
5. **Canonical multi-Rees interpretation.** No canonical physical role is assigned to possible multi-Rees refinements.
6. **Experimental robustness.** Noise, dissipation and parameter uncertainty are outside this release.
7. **Paper II publication metadata.** Paper I's DOI and repository are verified. A separate Paper II DOI and final affiliation/ORCID information remain `UNAVAILABLE`.
