## Reuse audit

The integrated manuscript was newly drafted from theorem statements and exact data. No paragraph from Paper I was copied verbatim. Standard mathematical strings such as the matrix, formulas, theorem names and definitions are necessarily identical where the object is locked. Those formula-level repetitions are recorded in `FORMULA_SOURCE_MAP.csv`.

The old R1.2 exposition was not pasted wholesale. Its exact numerator and fixed-ray result were re-proved, its 12-section organisation was replaced by the required 14-section architecture, and its evidence/workflow vocabulary was not imported.
