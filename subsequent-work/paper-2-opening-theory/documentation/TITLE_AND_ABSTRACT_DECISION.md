# Title and abstract decision

## Selected title

**Exact Opening and Coefficient-Sensitive Newton Geometry of a Six-State Dark Quantum Channel**

This title is retained because the manuscript now contains exact opening results beyond the fixed-ray theorem and because “coefficient-sensitive” signals the central aggregation/cancellation issue. “Quantum channel” is used descriptively for the declared source--target Hamiltonian entry, not as a claim about a completely positive map.

## Conservative fallback

**Exact Opening and Newton-Face Classification for a Six-State Diagonal Transfer Family**

Use the fallback if a target journal treats “quantum channel” as reserved terminology.

## Abstract audit

The abstract:

- states the locked numerator and the two dark planes;
- distinguishes exact computation from claimed generality;
- includes endpoint, pullback, analytic-arc, MV1 and GF1 results;
- contains the corrected fixed-weight theorem and its non-uniform limitation;
- avoids priority superlatives, experimental claims and unproved robustness language.
