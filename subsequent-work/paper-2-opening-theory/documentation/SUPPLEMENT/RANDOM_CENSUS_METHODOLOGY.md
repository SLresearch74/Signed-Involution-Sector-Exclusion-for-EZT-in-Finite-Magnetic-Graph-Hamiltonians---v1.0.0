# Random endpoint census methodology

The EA1 source release includes a finite diagnostic census of small labelled systems. Its retained count is `MEASURED` with **n=256**. The census was designed to exercise endpoint-unreachable labels, multiple endpoint-minimal words, cancellation of boundary words, non-dark baselines, scalar-ray collisions and module-valued outputs.

The census is not rerun as part of the integrated theorem certificate because the general endpoint theorem has a direct labelled-word proof. No frequency from the census is presented as a probability or performance estimate.
