# Notation and terminology report

The manuscript is consistent with `NOTATION_LOCK.md`.

- **exact dark / exact zero transfer:** means the source--target amplitude is identically zero in time;
- **opening:** requires an explicitly non-zero coefficient;
- **certificate:** a sufficient proof mechanism, not the definition of darkness;
- **support boundary:** an allowed exponent layer; it becomes sharp only after the boundary sum is non-zero;
- **candidate face:** the graded pullback before aggregation cancellation is tested;
- **actual initial face:** the first non-zero child homogeneous component;
- **fixed fibre:** one direction chosen independently of the scalar perturbation;
- **moving arc:** an analytic direction depending on a separate parameter;
- **coefficient-stratified decision:** a finite algebraic partition in the leading constants at a fixed weight;
- **global atlas:** a weight-uniform finite recursive classification, which is not proved.

The term **quantum channel** is explicitly used for a source--target Hamiltonian transfer channel, not for a completely positive map. A conservative title fallback is supplied.
