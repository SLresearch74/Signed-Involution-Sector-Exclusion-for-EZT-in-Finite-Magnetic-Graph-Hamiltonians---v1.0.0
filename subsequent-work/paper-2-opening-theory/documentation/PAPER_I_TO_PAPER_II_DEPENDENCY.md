# Paper I to Paper II dependency

Paper I is **Signed-Involution Sector Exclusion for Exact Zero Transfer in Finite Magnetic Graph Hamiltonians** (Medford, June 2026), version `v1.0.0`, DOI [`10.5281/zenodo.20556571`](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20556571). Paper II depends on exactly three pieces of it:

1. for a finite Hamiltonian, exact zero transfer is equivalent to vanishing of every source--target moment;
2. the same condition is equivalent to target orthogonality to the source Krylov space and to the corresponding spectral-projector condition;
3. a signed involution can be a sufficient exact-zero certificate, but losing that certificate does not prove opening.

Paper II does not reproduce Paper I's graph atlas, its 34 certificate examples, magnetic-flux examples, norm bounds, mass discussion or Lindblad extensions. The only repeated model-independent definition is the finite-dimensional transfer amplitude needed to make Paper II readable on its own.

Paper II's independent content begins with the locked six-state matrix and its four internal diagonal coordinates. The new objects are the exact coefficient ideal, endpoint-access words, fixed-ray and moving-arc Newton geometry, monomial pullback aggregation, the actual-coordinate 15-cell atlas, the 16-cone dark-ideal fan, Rees/contact data, marked sections and fixed-weight coefficient-stratified termination.

Paper I is cited through its verified Zenodo DOI. Its public source repository is [`SLresearch74/Signed-Involution-Sector-Exclusion-for-EZT-in-Finite-Magnetic-Graph-Hamiltonians---v1.0.0`](https://github.com/SLresearch74/Signed-Involution-Sector-Exclusion-for-EZT-in-Finite-Magnetic-Graph-Hamiltonians---v1.0.0). Paper II remains a clearly labelled subsequent-work subtree and does not change the formal status of Paper I.
