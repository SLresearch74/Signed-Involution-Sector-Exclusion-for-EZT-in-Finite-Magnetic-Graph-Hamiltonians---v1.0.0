# Block-generator taxonomy and local-certificate obstruction

## General complete rule

For a reachable one-dimensional block, g is the normalized content of its entire finite marked future. This is PROVED-SYNTHESIS under the frozen denominator, horizon, properness and stratum hypotheses. A direct-readout test alone is not complete. Computing each arrow ideal before coherent propagation is also not complete.

The useful certificate hierarchy is: inspect direct Song/readout contractions; test exact signed forward-closure identities; search for early unit or common-divisor witnesses; use the finite arithmetic recurrence when these shortcuts do not decide the case. The last step makes the hierarchy complete but does not justify calling its input a smaller combinatorial signature.

## Forward-closed zero theorem — PROVED-SYNTHESIS

Let Z be a set of reachable coordinate blocks. If Omega vanishes identically on Z and every declared nonzero arrow with source in Z has target in Z, then every finite and infinite residual vanishes on Z. Proof: for r_0=Omega and r_(h+1,gamma)=sum_delta r_(h,delta)M_(delta,gamma), induct on h. Terms leaving Z vanish by hypothesis; terms remaining in Z vanish by induction. Therefore the blocks in Z have g=0.

This is sufficient, not necessary for g=0. It is a coordinate-closure certificate and avoids assuming that coherent cancellation is impossible. Once K0 is known, its greatest forward-closed subset under the *declared* arrows is Kmax. That separate operation cannot be used as a definition of K0.

## Exact counterexample to local necessity — formal, not graph certified

Use three one-dimensional states A,B,C, Omega=(0,1/z,1/z), and only arrows A->B=1/z, A->C=-1/z. Then r_(0,A)=0, r_(1,A)=1/z^2-1/z^2=0, and all higher grades vanish because the transition square is zero. Thus g_A=0 although both outgoing arrows lead to directly observed states. K0=span(e_A), but Kmax=0. Replacing the negative arrow by +1/z gives g_A=1 with exactly the same local zero pattern and unweighted incidence.

This falsifies the proposed local iff in the class of signed scalar recurrences. It is not evidence that this exact three-state control is an oriented-circulant realization. A universal graph-only necessity theorem would require an additional realizability argument; it is not claimed here.

## Solved graph-realized subclass

For the certified dark-pair one-event class,

\[
r_{h,\gamma}=a b^h\rho^h q_\gamma,\qquad g_\gamma=\operatorname{norm}_\Sigma(a).
\]

Grade zero already has sharp content (a); all future grades are divisible by a. This is a compact classifier with no horizon growth. It applies to arbitrary polynomial amplitudes satisfying (a,b)A_Sigma=A_Sigma, not just the two affine examples.

Taking a=f(t),b=1 realizes any prescribed normalized polynomial f as every block generator on the same connected n=8 graph, with constant coefficient and projected ranks. The family remains rank one even at every root of f. Taking f=0 or 1 realizes the other two classes. Hence there is no prime-power-only restriction to generators 0 and 1 in the permitted moving-ket class, and no amplitude-blind static Song signature determines g.
