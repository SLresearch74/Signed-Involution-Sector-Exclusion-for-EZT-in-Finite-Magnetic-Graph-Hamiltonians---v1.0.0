# Zero / unit / nonconstant classification

Scope: **one-dimensional rank-one symbolic parameter families over fixed prime-power Song arithmetic**, on fixed strong architecture/reachability strata. The response field, locally closed stratum and zero-generator repairs remain mandatory.

## Complete terminal classification — PROVED-SYNTHESIS

Let C_gamma be the finite coefficient list of the exact full-future numerator, and C_e the coefficient list of the coherently recombined arrow numerator. For either list C, form its ideal on the stratum before interpreting a generator.

| Class | Necessary and sufficient certificate on a PID domain stratum | Geometric locus |
|---|---|---|
| zero | Every coefficient is zero in the stratum ring | All of Sigma |
| unit | The coefficient ideal is the unit ideal; equivalently a Bezout identity exists | Empty |
| nonconstant | The list is nonzero and has a nonunit common divisor after removing inverted factors | Proper locus V(g) or V(h) intersect Sigma |

For blocks, the finite horizon must be justified by the frozen realization. For edges no future horizon is involved. A nonzero constant in K is always a unit, regardless of its magnitude or cyclotomic complexity. Geometry is over an algebraic closure unless a base-field restriction is explicitly stated.

## Smaller certificates, in useful order

For unit g, search first for a unit direct-readout coefficient, then a grade of unit content; if neither exists, a cross-grade Bezout identity can still prove the unit class. For zero g, a direct readout zero alone is not enough: use an identically zero row restricted to a forward-closed coordinate set, or an exact signed cancellation identity for every future, or finally the complete finite coefficient test. For nonconstant g, exhibit divisibility through the full horizon and a sharp coefficient/gcd witness ruling out additional factors.

There is no proved universally smallest local certificate. The finite coefficient test is complete; early certificates are sufficient shortcuts. A terminating hierarchy that falls back to that test is a valid classification procedure but is not itself a universal compact signature.

## Three distinct zero mechanisms

1. Local arithmetic zero: an individual fixed Song contraction vanishes, certified by the actual mask and displacement.
2. Coherent zero: nonzero contributions cancel after their declared signs, phases, grades and incidences have been retained.
3. Parameter-locus zero: the nonzero coherent coefficient list has a common nonunit polynomial factor.

These mechanisms can overlap in provenance, but they must not all be called Song zeros. On fixed K, a nonzero Song value has no nonconstant parameter factor.
