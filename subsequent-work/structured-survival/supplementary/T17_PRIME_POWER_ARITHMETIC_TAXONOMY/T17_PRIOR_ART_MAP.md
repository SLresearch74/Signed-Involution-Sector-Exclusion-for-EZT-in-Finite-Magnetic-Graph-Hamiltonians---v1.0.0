## Priority question: is the finite-future content test standard?

Yes at the mechanism level. For a fixed finite linear realization with transition M and readout Omega, a coordinate e_gamma is unobservable iff Omega M^h e_gamma=0 through a sufficient finite horizon. Clearing a parameter-independent rational denominator makes this a finite list of polynomial equations. Over a univariate PID the ideal generated by that list is represented by a gcd. Its zero locus is exactly simultaneous coefficient vanishing. This is our mathematical deduction combining standard observability and content facts; it is not evidence of a new general systems theorem. The parametric-LTI certificate literature makes the novelty risk especially direct.

The part worth presenting as PROVED-SYNTHESIS is the implementation of that mechanism in the fixed marked Song/grade endpoint architecture, distinguishing decomposable K0 from individually declared-arrow survival Kmax, with graph-certified parameter loci and an amplitude-blind obstruction. Whether the exact combined synthesis or the dark-pair construction has priority is OPEN.

## Important non-equivalences

Classical input/output decoupling zeros often refer to roots in the spectral variable z; here g and h are in the physical parameter t and describe vanishing identically in z through the marked future. Related polynomial matrix gcd methods are close but should not be described as literally identical without matching these quantifiers. Ordinary unobservability is aggregate; Kmax additionally demands invariance under each declared arrow. Generic structural observability of a free sparsity pattern is also not the same as exact coherent cancellation with constrained Song coefficients.
